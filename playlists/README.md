## Platform policy

For early historical material, a platform playlist is considered complete only when the chosen item is the intended period recording or a clearly documented proxy. A title match alone is not enough.

YouTube may therefore be the authoritative playlist for the earliest chapters, with TIDAL containing the closest catalogue-complete subset where exact recordings are unavailable.
